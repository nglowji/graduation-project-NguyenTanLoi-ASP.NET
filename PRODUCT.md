# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- **Sports Players**: People looking for a pitch to book. They value speed, proximity, and price clarity.
- **Pitch Owners**: Managing their facilities and viewing how they appear to users.

## Product Purpose
A premium, easy-to-use sports pitch booking platform that connects players with facilities seamlessly.

## Brand Personality
- **Efficient**: Everything is one click away.
- **Modern**: Sleek dark/light modes with vibrant accents.
- **Reliable**: Trustworthy data and clear visual cues.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered, ad-heavy sports news sites.
- Generic, bland administrative forms.
- Confusing, multi-step filtering processes.

## Design Principles
- **Efficiency First**: The fastest path to a booking is the best design.
- **Visual Precision**: Use high contrast and sharp typography to convey data.
- **Feedback & Motion**: Every interaction should feel alive and responsive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance.
- Support for screen readers and high-contrast modes.
- Responsive design for all screen sizes.
